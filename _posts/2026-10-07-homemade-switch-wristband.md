## Project description

For this project, I made a felt wristband with an LED that only lights up when two switches are closed at the same time. I used two different homemade switches. The first is a pair of metal snaps that also hold the wristband together when I wear it. The second is a paperclip that I press against a piece of conductive fabric to complete the circuit.

The circuit works as one continuous loop. The positive side of the battery connects to the LED which then connects to the paperclip. From there the conductive fabric connects to the back snap and the front snap connects back to the negative side of the battery. Both switches have to be closed for the LED to turn on. If either one is open, the LED stays off.

I kept the battery holder and conductive thread traces on the back so the front would look cleaner. On the front, you can see the LED, the paperclip switch and the snap. I also added pink stitches around the LED to make it look like a sun with rays coming out of it. I thought this made the wristband look more interesting than just having an LED in the middle.

## Tip for anyone trying this

I would recommend taking your time when sewing the conductive thread. It helps to work on one connection at a time and test it with alligator clips before moving on. Make sure to cut off any loose thread ends because they can accidentally touch another connection and short the circuit. Also, check that the side of the conductive fabric touching the paperclip actually conducts electricity. Testing these small things along the way can help you later.
