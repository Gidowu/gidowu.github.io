Welcome to my corner of the internet! Since this is my first real post, here's the rundown on who I am and what I'm bringing (or not bringing) into Electronic Textiles this semester.

### The basics
I'm **Godwin Idowu**, a **senior** studying **Math and Computing**. I like problems that have clean, provable answers — which is exactly why this class is going to stretch me in a good way.

### Where I'm starting from
- **Sewing experience:** Zero. None. I have never threaded a needle in my life, so my first seam is going to be an event.
- **Programming experience:** Comfortable. I've written plenty of code across a few languages and I'm at home debugging logic — this is the part of the class I expect to lean on.
- **Circuitry/Arduino experience:** A little. I've wired up an LED or two and poked around with a microcontroller, but I wouldn't call myself dangerous with a breadboard yet.

### What I hope to get out of this class
Honestly? I want to learn how to build a "circuit board" out of thread. More specifically, I want to walk away knowing how to turn an idea into a soft, wearable prototype — something that lights up, senses, or reacts — sewn together with my own hands instead of soldered onto a board. Bonus goal: prove that a math major can also be crafty.

### One boring fact about me
I can solve a quadratic equation in under 15 seconds. It has come up in conversation exactly never but I stand ready.

Follow along as I (hopefully) go from "has never sewn a stitch" to "made a thing that blinks when you wear it."
