### One tip for Past Me
Treat your thread length like variable scope: keep it short enough that you don't lose track of what it's doing three stitches later. A long thread feels efficient at first — fewer re-threads! — but it tangles into technical debt fast (see: the yellow knot cluster above, which is basically an unhandled exception).

Also, unlike code, embroidery has no `Ctrl+Z`. Lightly pencil your fill areas before you satin-stitch into them — there's no `git commit --amend` for thread.
