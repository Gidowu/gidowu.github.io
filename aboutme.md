### My story
I enjoy building apps and experimenting with new ideas — this site is part of that, as I work through Computing 103 (Electronic Textiles) and put together a portfolio of what I'm learning along the way.

### Why Electronic Textiles?
Hi, I'm Godwin — a senior studying Math and Computing who's spent most of my time thinking in code and equations, not thread and fabric. I'm taking Electronic Textiles because I think the next generation of interesting interfaces won't just live on screens — they'll live in what we wear. I want to take the logic I already know from software and circuits and learn to express it physically: soft, flexible, and worn on the body instead of held in a hand. My goal this semester is to build something that proves computing doesn't have to feel like a computer — it can feel like a jacket, a bracelet, a seam. If you're curious what happens when a math major picks up a needle for the first time, follow along here.
