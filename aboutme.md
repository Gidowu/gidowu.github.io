### My story
I enjoy building apps and experimenting with new ideas — this site is part of that, as I work through Computing 103 (Electronic Textiles) and put together a portfolio of what I'm learning along the way.

### Why Electronic Textiles?
Have you ever noticed how much of "smart" technology still lives trapped behind a screen? I'm Godwin, a senior studying Math and Computing — I've spent years writing code and proving theorems, but almost none of that logic has ever touched something soft. That's exactly why I'm taking Electronic Textiles: I want to take what I already know about circuits and computation and build it into things people actually wear, not just carry. My goal this semester is simple — take one idea from "blinking cursor" to "blinking sleeve," and prove that a math nerd can pick up a needle and make something that thinks.
